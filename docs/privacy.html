What Sprout is

Sprout is a dictation and Vibe Coding helper. A floating bubble records your voice and inserts text into the app you are using. Speech-to-text and prompt generation run on your phone after a one-time model download. There is no hosted Flow backend in the Play listing.

Data we process

DataWhyWhere
Microphone audio Speech-to-text for dictation Processed on-device with Whisper; not uploaded after models are installed
Focused text field contents Vibe prompt and grammar correction Processed on-device with Gemma; not uploaded
Model files (Whisper, Gemma) On-device inference Downloaded once over HTTPS into app-private storage

The Android app does not require a flow-api URL or API key.

Permissions

  • Microphone — record dictation audio after an in-app disclosure
  • Display over other apps — floating control bubble
  • Accessibility — read the focused editable field and insert results. Not used to scrape unrelated screen content
  • Internet — first-run download of on-device model files only
  • Notifications — foreground service status while the bubble runs

What we do not do

  • No account system
  • No analytics SDK
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No sale of audio or transcripts
  • No reading of non-editable or non-focused UI beyond finding the active field
  • No cloud STT/LLM calls from the Android client

Retention

The Android client does not keep recordings. Audio and field text live only long enough to run on-device inference. Downloaded model files stay in app storage until you clear app data or uninstall.
